# Privacy Policy — AUTO-D Kenya

**Last updated: 28 August 2026**

AUTO-D Kenya ("AUTO-D," "we," "us," or "our") is a vehicle intelligence platform providing vehicle valuation, mileage-cost analysis, running-cost analysis, total cost of ownership tools, and related vehicle information for the Kenyan market.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you use the AUTO-D Kenya website, Android application, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create an AUTO-D account, we may collect:
- Email address
- Password or authentication credentials
- Name and other profile information that you voluntarily provide

Where you use a third-party sign-in option such as Google or Facebook, we may receive information made available to us by that provider, such as your name, email address, and account identifier, subject to that provider's privacy policies and your account settings.

Passwords are processed through our authentication provider and are not stored by AUTO-D in plain text.

### 1.2 Vehicle Information

To provide our valuation and vehicle-cost services, we may collect information you enter or submit about a vehicle, including:
- Registration number
- Make and model
- Model year
- Mileage
- Fuel type
- Transmission
- Body type
- Vehicle condition
- Purchase price or estimated purchase price
- Location or other information relevant to valuation
- Other vehicle information necessary to generate a report

We may use this information to calculate estimated vehicle values, mileage costs, running costs, ownership costs, and related reports.

### 1.3 Payment Information

Where you purchase a service through M-Pesa, we may collect information necessary to identify and confirm the transaction, including:
- M-Pesa phone number
- Transaction amount
- Transaction date and time
- M-Pesa transaction/reference number
- Payment status
- Service purchased

M-Pesa payments are processed through Safaricom's applicable payment infrastructure, including the Daraja API.

AUTO-D does **not** request, collect, or store your M-Pesa PIN.

Where Google Play Billing is used for purchases made through the Android application, payment processing is handled by Google Play. AUTO-D does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

### 1.4 Reports and Service Information

We may retain the valuation reports, mileage calculations, ownership-cost calculations, and other results generated through your use of the Service.

### 1.5 Automatically Collected Information

When you access the Service, we may automatically collect limited technical and usage information, including:
- IP address
- Approximate location derived from IP address
- Device type
- Operating system
- Browser or application information
- Pages or screens visited
- Date and time of access
- Referring website or application
- Basic diagnostic and performance information

Where analytics tools are enabled, this information may be processed by third-party analytics providers such as Google Analytics.

### 1.6 Cookies and Local Storage

AUTO-D may use cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar technologies to:
- Maintain authentication sessions
- Remember user preferences
- Maintain login state
- Remember whether a service or report has been unlocked
- Improve security and performance
- Understand how users interact with the Service

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some functionality of the Service.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate users
- Provide vehicle valuation services
- Generate mileage-cost and running-cost calculations
- Generate total cost of ownership reports
- Process and verify payments
- Provide access to purchased services and reports
- Send payment confirmations and other service-related communications
- Respond to customer enquiries and support requests
- Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Monitor and improve the performance and functionality of the Service
- Conduct analytics and understand usage patterns
- Maintain appropriate business, accounting, tax, and transaction records
- Comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, and lawful requests from government authorities

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, AUTO-D processes personal information on the following lawful bases:

| **Processing Activity** | **Lawful Basis** |
| :--- | :--- |
| Account creation, authentication, and service delivery | **Performance of a Contract** — necessary to provide the Service you have requested |
| M-Pesa payment processing and transaction verification | **Performance of a Contract** and **Legal Obligation** — to fulfill our service and meet financial record-keeping requirements |
| Service-related communications (confirmations, support) | **Performance of a Contract** and **Legitimate Interests** — to communicate essential information |
| Fraud prevention, security monitoring, and system improvement | **Legitimate Interests** — to ensure security and proper functioning, not overridden by your rights |
| Tax, accounting, and regulatory compliance | **Legal Obligation** — to comply with applicable Kenyan laws |
| Analytics and usage understanding | **Legitimate Interests** — to improve the Service, subject to appropriate safeguards |

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

AUTO-D uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction.

These measures include:
- HTTPS/TLS encrypted communications

However, no internet transmission, electronic storage system, or security measure can guarantee absolute security.
